# Landing page

Use the [landing-page guide](../../../docs/coding-standards/landing-pages/README.md)
for the requested work. It owns the method, content and visual decisions,
review, and scope of delivery. Read it before drafting or revising the page.

Follow that guide instead of the fixed page form and cuts of
[plain-writing](../plain-writing/SKILL.md). The guide links the shared writing
standard and knowledge workflow; do not create a second copy of their rules.
