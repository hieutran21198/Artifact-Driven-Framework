The structure

  1. GoalA need, and how to check it is met.A member borrows a book.
  2. FlowThe steps, in order.Borrowing, returning.
  3. RuleWhat must stay true.Only a book on the shelf can be lent.
  4. ArtifactOne topic and its rules.Books, members, notices.
  5. AppWhere people do it.Website, phone app.

Borrowing a book

Situations

  1. A rule changes

    List what relies on the rule.

    BK-3, "only a book on the shelf": both flows and the phone app.

  2. A tester reports a defect

    Find the rule it breaks. Compare it with the product.

    Defect: a book on loan was lent again. Rule: BK-3, on the shelf only.

  3. Someone joins the team

    Open the Books page.

    10 rules, BK-1 to BK-10.

  4. A release is near

    Look at the rules with no test.

    "A book not back in time is marked overdue": no test yet.

  5. A stakeholder asks what has tests

    Project tracker: the share of each goal's rules with a test.

    Open the project tracker

  6. The docs drift from the code

    Docs check: fails a broken link, an unknown ID, or a rule citing a flow.

    A flow citing BK-11 fails: no such rule.
